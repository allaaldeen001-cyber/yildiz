⚡ QUICK REFERENCE:

Upload Firmware:
  1. Install libraries (LIBRARIES.txt)
  2. Upload RemoteController.ino to RC Arduino
  3. Upload FlightController.ino to FC Arduino

Pin Mapping (Flight Controller):
  Motors: D3 (FL), D5 (FR), D6 (RR), D9 (RL)
  Radio: CE=D4, CSN=D10, SPI=D11-D13
  Sensors: I2C=A4/A5 (MPU6050 + MS5611)
  LED: D7, Buzzer: D8

Pin Mapping (Remote Controller):
  Joysticks: A0 (Throttle), A1 (Yaw), A2 (Pitch), A3 (Roll)
  Buttons: D4-D7
  Switches: D2-D3
  Radio: CE=D9, CSN=D10, SPI=D11-D13

Flight Modes:
  ANGLE: SW2=ON, SW1=OFF (auto-level)
  ACRO: SW2=OFF, SW1=OFF (rate control)
  ALT HOLD: SW1=ON (altitude hold)
  TAKEOFF: Press Button 4 (auto rise to 1.5m)
  LANDING: Press Button 3 (auto descend)
