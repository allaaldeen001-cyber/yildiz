- [ ] All libraries installed

## 📧 Support

### If You Need Help

1. **Check documentation** for your specific issue
2. **Re-read** the relevant section carefully
3. **Verify wiring** against diagrams
4. **Test systematically** using troubleshooting guide
5. **Ask for help** with complete details:
   - What you're trying to do
   - What's happening instead
   - Error messages (Serial Monitor)
   - What you've tried already

## 🎉 Success Criteria

You'll know your build is successful when:

✅ Remote controller transmits reliably  
✅ Flight controller receives commands  
✅ All sensors initialize without errors  
✅ Motors spin in correct directions  
✅ Drone takes off smoothly (Button 4)  
✅ Hovers stably in ANGLE mode  
✅ Responds to stick inputs  
✅ Lands safely (Button 3)  
✅ No oscillations or vibrations  
✅ Radio failsafe works
