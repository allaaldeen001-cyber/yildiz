================================================================================
                          PIN CONFIGURATION
================================================================================

FLIGHT CONTROLLER (Arduino Nano):
   Motors:     D3=FL | D5=FR | D6=RR | D9=RL
   NRF24:      D2=CE | D10=CSN | D11-13=SPI
   Sensors:    A4=SDA | A5=SCL (MPU6050 + MS5611)
   Buttons:    A6=Calibrate | A7=Motor Start
   Switches:   D4=Arm | D7=Altitude Hold
   Outputs:    D8=Buzzer | A3=LED
   Monitor:    A0=Battery

REMOTE CONTROLLER (Arduino Nano):
   NRF24:      D9=CE | D10=CSN | D11-13=SPI
   Joysticks:  A0=Throttle | A1=Yaw | A2=Pitch | A3=Roll
   Output:     D8=Status LED (optional)

================================================================================
                         IMPORTANT NOTES
================================================================================

⚠️  CRITICAL - BEFORE YOU START:

   1. NRF24L01 MUST have 10µF capacitor between VCC & GND
   2. NRF24L01 uses 3.3V (NOT 5V!)
   3. Calibrate on perfectly level surface before EVERY flight
   4. Test motor directions WITHOUT propellers first
   5. Always fly in open areas away from people

📋 REQUIRED ARDUINO LIBRARIES:
   • RF24 (by TMRh20)
   • Smoothed (by Matthew Fryer)
   • MS5611 (by Rob Tillaart)
   • Wire, SPI, Servo, EEPROM (built-in)

🎮 MOTOR ROTATION PATTERN:
   Front Left (D3):   CCW ↺
   Front Right (D5):  CW  ↻
   Rear Right (D6):   CCW ↺
   Rear Left (D9):    CW  ↻
