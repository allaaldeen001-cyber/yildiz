# ⚠️ DRONE SAFETY MANUAL

## CRITICAL SAFETY RULES

### ⚡ Before You Start

**READ THIS ENTIRE DOCUMENT BEFORE FLYING!**

Drones are **NOT TOYS**. They contain:
- Sharp spinning propellers (up to 15,000 RPM)
- Powerful LiPo batteries (fire hazard if damaged)
- Fast-moving components that can cause serious injury

**Violation of these safety rules can result in:**
- Personal injury or death
- Property damage
- Legal consequences
- Fire or explosion

---

## 🔥 LITHIUM POLYMER (LiPo) BATTERY SAFETY

### Battery Rules
1. **NEVER charge unattended**
2. **NEVER charge on flammable surfaces**
3. **NEVER use damaged or swollen batteries**
4. **NEVER over-discharge below 3.0V per cell**
5. **ALWAYS store at 3.8V per cell (storage voltage)**
6. **ALWAYS use a LiPo bag when charging**
7. **ALWAYS balance charge**
8. **KEEP away from children and pets**

### Warning Signs
| Sign | Action |
|------|--------|
| Battery swollen/puffy | **DISPOSE IMMEDIATELY** |
| Battery hot to touch | **DISCONNECT AND COOL** |
| Smoke or smell | **EVACUATE AREA** |
| Hissing sound | **MOVE TO SAFE AREA** |

### Fire Response
1. **DO NOT USE WATER** (lithium fire)
2. Use class D fire extinguisher or sand
3. Evacuate area
4. Call emergency services

## 🚁 PROPELLER SAFETY

### Critical Rules
1. **ALWAYS remove propellers during testing/calibration**
2. **NEVER touch spinning propellers**
3. **NEVER reach over armed drone**
4. **ALWAYS disarm before approaching drone**
5. **CHECK propeller condition before each flight**

### Propeller Injuries
- Can cause **deep lacerations**
- Can cause **eye injuries** (wear safety glasses)
- Can cause **finger amputation**
- **Medical attention required** for propeller cuts

### Installation Safety
1. Match propeller type to motor rotation (CW/CCW)
2. Secure with propeller nut/bolt (use thread locker)
3. Check for cracks or chips
4. Ensure proper orientation
5. Test spin balance (no wobble)

## 🚨 EMERGENCY PROCEDURES

### Loss of Control
1. **IMMEDIATE**: Toggle kill switch to DISARM
2. Take cover if drone coming towards you
3. Allow drone to crash safely
4. Wait 30 seconds before approaching
5. Disconnect battery
6. Inspect for damage

### Signal Loss (Failsafe)
- Drone **automatically** reduces throttle
- Attempts controlled descent
- **DO NOT PANIC** - let failsafe work
- Be ready to use kill switch if needed
- Mark landing location

### Motor Failure
1. Activate kill switch immediately
2. Drone will fall - **clear area**
3. Do not attempt to catch falling drone
4. Wait for crash, then approach carefully
5. Inspect failed motor/ESC

### Battery Failure
- If battery smokes: **EVACUATE IMMEDIATELY**
- If battery sparks: **DO NOT TOUCH**
- If battery swells: **LAND IMMEDIATELY**
- Never attempt mid-flight battery fixes

### Flyaway (Uncontrolled Flight)
1. Toggle kill switch repeatedly
2. Note direction of flight
3. Mark last seen location
4. Search area carefully
5. Disable battery immediately when found

## 👥 SPECTATOR SAFETY

### If Others Are Present
1. **Designate safety perimeter** (10m minimum)
2. **Assign spotter** to watch for hazards
3. **Brief all observers** on emergency procedures
4. **Keep children and pets** far away
5. **Provide eye protection** to close observers

### Public Spaces
- **NEVER fly over people**
- **NEVER fly over vehicles**
- **NEVER fly near airports** (illegal, 5km+ minimum)
- **NEVER fly over stadiums or events**
- **CHECK local laws and regulations**

## 🏥 FIRST AID

### Propeller Cuts
1. **Stop bleeding** with direct pressure
2. **Clean wound** thoroughly
3. **Apply sterile bandage**
4. **Seek medical attention** (may need stitches)
5. **Update tetanus** if needed

### Burns (Battery Fire)
1. **Cool immediately** with water (20 minutes)
2. **Cover with sterile cloth**
3. **DO NOT apply ice directly**
4. **Seek medical attention** for serious burns

### Eye Injuries
1. **DO NOT rub eyes**
2. **Flush with clean water** for 15 minutes
3. **Seek immediate medical attention**
4. **Cover eye with sterile pad** (both eyes)

### Electrical Shock
1. **DO NOT touch victim** if still in contact
2. **Turn off power source**
3. **Call emergency services** (911)
4. **Begin CPR** if trained and needed

## 🧰 MAINTENANCE SAFETY

### Regular Inspections
**Before Every Flight:**
- [ ] Check all screws and bolts
- [ ] Inspect propellers for damage
- [ ] Test motor spin (by hand, DISARMED)
- [ ] Check battery voltage
- [ ] Verify kill switch operation

**Weekly:**
- [ ] Clean dust from electronics
- [ ] Check solder joints
- [ ] Inspect wire insulation
- [ ] Test ESC operation
- [ ] Calibrate IMU if needed

**Monthly:**
- [ ] Deep clean entire drone
- [ ] Replace worn propellers
- [ ] Test battery capacity
- [ ] Update firmware if needed
- [ ] Full calibration sequence

### Electrical Safety
1. **DISCONNECT battery** before working on drone
2. **AVOID short circuits** (remove jewelry, use insulated tools)
3. **CHECK polarity** before connecting power
4. **USE proper crimps** for all connections
5. **INSULATE exposed wires** with heat shrink

## 🌍 ENVIRONMENTAL SAFETY

### Responsible Flying
- Respect wildlife (birds may attack drone)
- Don't fly over water unless waterproof
- Retrieve crashed drones (don't litter)
- Dispose of batteries properly
- Consider noise pollution

### Weather Restrictions
**DO NOT FLY IN:**
- Rain or fog
- High winds (>15km/h)
- Thunderstorms or lightning
- Extreme temperatures (<0°C or >40°C)
- Poor visibility

## 📋 ACCIDENT REPORTING

### If Accident Occurs
1. **Ensure safety** of all people first
2. **Provide first aid** if needed
3. **Document incident** (photos, notes)
4. **Preserve evidence** (drone, logs)
5. **Report to authorities** if required
6. **Review and learn** from incident

### Incident Log
Keep record of:
- Date, time, location
- Weather conditions
- What went wrong
- Injuries or damage
- Corrective actions taken
