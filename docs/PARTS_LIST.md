## Budget Savings Tips

1. **Buy in Bulk**: Get 2+ drones worth, share with friends
2. **Use Coupons**: AliExpress often has $2-5 off coupons
3. **Wait for Sales**: Black Friday, 11.11, etc.
4. **Bundle Deals**: Frame+motor+ESC kits save 20-30%
5. **Generic Brands**: Work fine for learning, upgrade later
6. **Used/Salvage**: Check local RC clubs for used parts

---

## Quality Warning: Avoid These

- ❌ NRF24L01 **without** PA+LNA (short range)
- ❌ Arduino Nano clones with bad CH340 chip (upload issues)
- ❌ LiPo batteries without balance connector
- ❌ ESCs without BLHeli firmware (poor performance)
- ❌ Mismatched propellers (CW/CCW not clearly marked)

## Shipping & Import Notes

### Shipping Times
- **China to USA/EU**: 15-30 days (standard)
- **Express Shipping**: 7-15 days (+$10-20)
- **Amazon Prime**: 1-2 days (higher price)

### Customs & Duties
- **Under $800 (USA)**: Usually no import duty
- **Under €150 (EU)**: May have VAT
- **Check local regulations**

### Batteries
- ⚠️ **LiPo batteries cannot ship by air in some countries**
- May need separate order with ground shipping
- Consider buying locally
