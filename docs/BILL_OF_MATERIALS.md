## 🔄 Alternative/Compatible Parts

### Arduino Nano Alternatives
- **Arduino Pro Mini** ($2-3) - Smaller, needs USB adapter
- **ESP32** ($5-8) - More powerful, WiFi/Bluetooth
- **STM32 Bluepill** ($3-5) - 32-bit ARM processor

### NRF24L01 Alternatives
- **NRF24L01+ (basic)** ($1) - Shorter range (~100m)
- **HC-12** ($3-5) - Longer range, different protocol

### MPU6050 Alternatives
- **MPU9250** ($3-5) - 9-axis (adds magnetometer)
- **ICM20602** ($3-4) - Better performance

### MS5611 Alternatives
- **BMP280** ($1-2) - Cheaper, slightly less accurate
- **BMP388** ($3-5) - Higher accuracy

### Motor Alternatives
- **2204 2300KV** - 5" props, good for 250-330mm frames
- **2212 1000KV** - 9-10" props, larger/slower drones
- **1806 2400KV** - Small/light racing drones

## 💡 Cost-Saving Tips

1. **Buy from China** (AliExpress/Banggood) - 50-70% cheaper, but slower shipping
2. **Buy in bulk** - Arduino Nanos, LEDs, resistors cheaper in 5-10 packs
3. **Combo deals** - Motor+ESC bundles save money
4. **Clone boards** - Arduino clones work fine (ensure CH340 driver)
5. **Wait for sales** - Black Friday, 11.11, etc.
6. **Local makerspaces** - May have tools to borrow
7. **Salvage parts** - Old RC cars/drones for motors, ESCs
8. **Skip non-essentials** - Can fly without barometer (no altitude hold)

---

## ⚠️ Don't Cheap Out On:

1. **LiPo Battery** - Cheap batteries can be dangerous (fire risk!)
2. **LiPo Charger** - Buy quality charger with balance charging
3. **Frame** - Weak frame = crashes = broken parts
4. **ESCs** - Bad ESCs can burn out motors
